# Privacy Policy — PaddleBeam Arcade

Effective date: August 6, 2026

PaddleBeam Arcade is developed by Chysti Alex.

## Data used by the app

PaddleBeam Arcade works offline and does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal data. It does not require an account and does not access contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.

The app stores only the player's high score in local app storage on the device. This value is not transmitted off the device. It can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

The optional “Support the developer” button opens an external PayPal page in the user's browser. Support is voluntary and does not unlock features, remove ads, or provide digital benefits. PayPal processes activity on its own service under PayPal's policies; the app does not receive or store payment information.

## Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

## Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before release.
